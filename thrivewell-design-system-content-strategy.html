Design system and content strategy

Thrivewell's website operating system.

A practical brand, interface and content system for a regulated home-care provider serving families across West Lothian, Edinburgh and East Lothian.

Audience: adult children choosing care for a parent, plus older adults, family carers and referral partners.

Conversion goal: request a free care assessment, call for advice or complete a care-needs check.

Strategic rule: every page must reduce anxiety, prove trust and make the next step obvious.

01 Foundations

Brand posture

Thrivewell should feel calm, regulated and easy to act on. The design language uses the approved clean functional system: BDO Grotesk, green-black text, warm raised surfaces, generous spacing jumps and rounded components. The healthcare adaptation is softer in content, but strict in interaction and accessibility.

Promise

Live well at home

Support older adults to remain safe, independent and treated with dignity in familiar surroundings.

Proof

Regulated and nurse-led

Care Inspectorate registration, nurse-led management, PVG checks, SSSC registration and 24/7 on-call support.

Tone

Warm, direct, practical

Speak plainly to families under pressure. Avoid sentimental copy, fear-based selling and invented outcomes.

Layout

Spacing and structure

Use the extracted scale without one-off spacing. Major page rhythm should rely on 60, 120, 160 and 200px. Cards and controls should use 12, 16, 20, 24 and 32px. This gives the brand a calm editorial pace without decorative excess.

Spacing tokens

1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 60, 120, 160, 200px.

Use 120px+ only for page-level rhythm, not component internals.

Radius tokens

8px for small controls, 12px for compact inputs, 16px for buttons and cards, 20px for panels, 24px for major sections.

Do not mix multiple radii inside the same component.

03 Content strategy

Messaging system

Content should guide a worried family from concern to action. The site should avoid broad claims like "best care" and instead explain what happens, who delivers care, how safety is monitored and how families can start.

Pillar 1

Safety

Regulated care, trained carers, PVG checks, SSSC registration, medication records and 24/7 on-call support.

Pillar 2

Dignity

Personal routines, privacy, choice, independence, respectful personal care and continuity where possible.

Pillar 3

Local trust

West Lothian, Livingston, Bathgate, Linlithgow, Edinburgh and East Lothian pages with unique local proof.

Core copy

Approved direction

Use this language as the starting point for the homepage and paid-search landing pages.

Homepage hero

Headline: Nurse-led home care that helps your parent stay safe, independent and well at home.

Subhead: Thrivewell Care provides regulated care at home across West Lothian, Edinburgh and East Lothian, with support for daily routines, medication, companionship, nights, respite and more complex needs.

Primary CTA: Arrange a free care assessment. Secondary CTA: Call for advice today.

Voice rules

How Thrivewell should sound

The writing should be plain, reassuring and action-oriented. It should acknowledge family pressure without dramatizing it. Sentences should be short enough to scan, with practical nouns and specific next steps.

Use

  • "Arrange a free care assessment"
  • "Support with washing, dressing and getting ready for the day"
  • "A care plan reviewed as needs change"
  • "Care at home in Livingston and nearby areas"
  • "Call us if you are unsure what level of care is needed"

Avoid

  • "World-class elderly care"
  • "The best carers in Scotland"
  • "Affordable care for everyone"
  • "Let us transform your life"
  • "High-quality services tailored to your needs" without specifics

05 QA

Acceptance checklist

Use this before any page is approved. These are pass/fail checks, not suggestions.

  • Every page must have one clear primary CTA above the fold.
  • Every service page must mention what the care includes and who it helps.
  • Every location page must include unique local copy, not duplicated town swaps.
  • Every component must define hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading and error where relevant.
  • All interactive targets must be at least 44 by 44px.
  • Normal text must meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements.
  • Forms must keep visible labels and text-based error messages.
  • Compliance claims must be specific and should link to a proof or trust page.
  • Copy must avoid invented statistics and unverifiable superiority claims.
  • Body copy should stay around 60-75 characters per line on desktop.
  • Cards must not be nested inside other cards.
  • Primary actions should use text-primary background with text-inverse copy.
  • Blog posts must link to a relevant service, location or trust page.
  • Footer must expose all legal and compliance support pages.

Final system rule

Trust before polish.

The design system exists to make the site feel calm and professional. The content strategy exists to make families confident enough to act. If a design decision makes the page prettier but less clear, it should be removed.
