Planning document

A conversion-led care website built around trust, locality and family reassurance.

This document extracts the usable content from the existing Thrivewell Care site and turns it into a full website content system, SEO sitemap and original visual direction for adult children choosing care for a parent.

Prepared May 27, 2026 · Source reviewed: thrivewellcare.lovable.app · Visual system updated from the attached clean functional design kit, adapted for Thrivewell Care.

Core strategy: make the next step feel safe. Every major page should answer three anxieties: Is this care regulated? Will my parent be treated with dignity? Can I speak to someone today?

The recommended system now uses a restrained BDO Grotesk-led visual language, plain-English healthcare copy and local SEO architecture.

Thrivewell Care already has the raw ingredients of a strong care-at-home brand: a clear mission, a nurse-led management structure, Care Inspectorate registration, PVG-checked and SSSC-registered carers, 24/7 on-call support and a geographic focus across West Lothian, Edinburgh and East Lothian. The current content also has a useful emotional thread: helping people live independently in the home they love while giving families peace of mind.

The website planning opportunity is not to add more decoration. It is to separate high-intent searches into dedicated pages, make each service and location page specific, and give families enough reassurance to contact the team without feeling rushed. Adult children comparing care providers are rarely browsing casually. They may be worried after a fall, a hospital discharge, early dementia symptoms, carer burnout or a parent no longer managing medication safely. The site should speak to those moments directly.

Source inventory and content assets

The existing site provides several content pillars that should be retained and developed. The strongest points are regulated care, nurse-led oversight, personal care planning, dignity, responsiveness and local coverage. The weaker point is architecture: the service offer is compressed into a small number of pages, which limits both SEO reach and conversion specificity.

  • Brand promise: help adults continue to live well, independently and with dignity, in their own home.
  • Operating region: West Lothian, including Livingston, Bathgate, Linlithgow, Whitburn, Armadale and surrounding areas, plus Edinburgh and East Lothian.
  • Trust proof: Care Inspectorate Scotland registered, nurse-led Registered Manager, PVG checks through Disclosure Scotland, SSSC registration and 24/7 on-call support.
  • Services currently visible: personal care, medication support, companionship, live-in care, respite care and sleeping or waking night care.
  • Additional requested pages: dementia care, post-hospital care, care assessment, funding guide, testimonials, Care Inspectorate page and legal policy pages.
The highest-converting page is not the prettiest page. It is the page that makes a worried family feel, within seconds, that calling is the sensible next step.

The top level should stay simple, but the second tier should be intentionally deep: individual service pages for care-intent searches, location pages for hyper-local intent and trust pages for families who need confidence before making contact. Service pages

Each service page should be built as a separate landing page because each answers a different search intent. "Live-in care West Lothian", "respite care Edinburgh" and "medication support Bathgate" are not the same decision. They deserve different headlines, proof points, FAQs and calls to action.

  1. /services/personal-care - bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting and continence support with dignity.
  2. /services/medication-support - reminders, prompting and administration documented through MAR charts.
  3. /services/companionship-care - conversation, outings, appointments, shopping and loneliness reduction.
  4. /services/live-in-care - a round-the-clock alternative to residential care for people who want to stay home.
  5. /services/respite-care - planned cover for family carers from a few hours to several weeks.
  6. /services/night-care - sleeping nights and waking nights for overnight reassurance or active support.
  7. /services/dementia-care - familiar routines, continuity, safety prompts and support for memory loss.
  8. /services/post-hospital-care - short-term settling-in support after discharge, falls or illness.

Location pages

The location pages should not be duplicated with town names swapped in. Each page needs a locally specific intro, nearby areas served, transport or community references where accurate, localised FAQs and testimonials where available. This is how the pages avoid thin-content risk while still targeting commercial searches.

  • /locations/west-lothian - county-level hub for care at home in West Lothian.
  • /locations/livingston - high-intent page for home care in Livingston and surrounding neighbourhoods.
  • /locations/bathgate - personal care, respite and medication support in Bathgate.
  • /locations/linlithgow - discreet care-at-home support with an emphasis on independence.
  • /locations/edinburgh - private home care and post-hospital support in Edinburgh.
  • /locations/east-lothian - companionship, live-in care and respite care across East Lothian.
Recommended location-page rule: at least 45 percent of the visible copy should be unique to that place. Include local coverage notes, common family situations and a location-specific CTA.

Copy system for the new site

The voice should be warm, direct and practical. Families should feel respected, not sold to. The site can be conversion-focused without becoming aggressive by using plain next steps: call, request a free care assessment, check care options or ask about funding. Avoid exaggerated claims and avoid invented outcomes.

Core message hierarchy

  1. Safety: regulated, nurse-led, trained, checked and supported 24/7.
  2. Dignity: care that protects routine, privacy, independence and choice.
  3. Locality: real care teams serving West Lothian, Edinburgh and East Lothian.
  4. Clarity: assessment, plan, matched carer, review and adjustment.
  5. Relief: families know who to call and what happens next.

Reusable service-page copy frame

  1. Situation-led opening: name the family concern in human language.
  2. What the service includes: practical tasks, boundaries and examples.
  3. Who it helps: adult children, older people, family carers or discharge teams.
  4. How Thrivewell delivers it: care plan, matched carer, documentation, review.
  5. Trust proof: Care Inspectorate, nurse-led oversight, PVG, SSSC, 24/7 support.
  6. Local CTA: "Book a free care assessment in [location]".

Updated design system recommendation

The visual system should use the attached clean functional kit as its foundation: BDO Grotesk typography, deep green-black text, raised warm surfaces, generous spacing jumps and rounded containers. For Thrivewell, that creates a calm, modern care brand that feels practical, accessible and easy for families to act on.

Foundation Rule
ColorUse #f3f5ee for the page surface, #f6f4f1 for raised cards, #1c2623 for text and primary actions, #dedcd6 for structure and #000000 for high-contrast dark sections.
TypographyUse BDO Grotesk across display, body and UI. Keep the scale disciplined: 12, 14, 16, 28, 40, 56 and 68px, with clear hierarchy from spacing and layout rather than heavy weights.
ComponentsButtons, links, cards, inputs and navigation must include default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading and error states before build handoff.
ImageryUse real local care photography when available; otherwise use abstract warm surface blocks and practical content diagrams until photography is commissioned.
AccessibilityTarget WCAG 2.2 AA, 44px touch targets, visible focus indicators, descriptive labels and no color-only communication.

Design-system handoff rules

Area Implementation guidance
TokensUse semantic names in build files: text-primary #1c2623, text-secondary #eceae1, text-tertiary #ffffff, text-inverse #f6f4f1, surface-base #000000, surface-raised #f3f5ee and surface-strong #dedcd6.
SpacingUse the extracted scale: 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 60, 120, 160 and 200px. Reserve 120px+ for major page rhythm, not card internals.
RadiiUse 8px for small controls, 12px for compact inputs, 16px for buttons and standard cards, 20px for larger panels and 24px for major sections.
ButtonsPrimary actions should use #1c2623 backgrounds with #f6f4f1 text. Secondary actions should use #dedcd6 with #1c2623 text. Every button must retain a 44px minimum target.
InputsLabels must stay visible. Focus-visible must be obvious. Error text must be descriptive and paired with programmatic state, not just a color change.
CardsCards should carry one job: service summary, trust proof, location entry or related content. Keep padding at 20-32px and avoid nested cards.
Content toneUse concise, confident, implementation-ready language. For family-facing pages, translate that into calm verbs: call, arrange, ask, check, plan and review.
